A price shock on Base reaches the pool on Unichain in 3 seconds.

Tremor carries the market price across chains with Reactive Omni, then charges arbitrage for trading against a stale pool. The extra fee stays with the people who supplied the liquidity.

How a price move travels

No bot, keeper or server sits in the middle. Each step is a contract reacting to the one before it.

  1. The market moves

    A trade in the tETH/tUSD pool and a price report from the feed each announce the new price.

    Base Sepolia

  2. Reactive checks it

    Tremor's reactive contract confirms both sources agree, scores how far it trusts the price, and sends it on.

    Reactive Lasna

  3. The pool prices the gap

    On every swap, the hook compares the pool price with the reference. Trades that exploit the gap pay a surcharge to LPs; everyone else pays the normal fee.

    Unichain Sepolia

What this test does not show

  • The market, the price feed and the arbitrage bots are our own scripts on testnet.
  • An arbitrageur that lands its trade in the 2 to 3 seconds before Tremor's update pays only the normal fee. A test bot that pre-signed its trades did exactly that on all 3 tries, landing in the same second as the market move; the experiment's slower bots never did.
  • 11 market moves is a small sample, limited by testnet funds. The runner is in the repository so anyone can run more.
